# Orient

Read the project index first. Review `status.md` for the immediate next action
and relevant recent entries in `log.md` for durable history. Identify the user's
intended outcome, assess whether the recorded plan still serves it, and surface
the most important risk or decision. Recommend one concrete next step.

Proceed with work authorized by a clear request, including ordinary supporting
steps and verification. Ask only when a missing choice materially affects the
outcome or authority. Keep recommendations distinct from agreed decisions;
a recorded next action alone does not authorize execution.
