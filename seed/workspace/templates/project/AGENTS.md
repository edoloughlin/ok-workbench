# <Project> instructions

Read the workspace-level `AGENTS.md` first, then this file. Add only the
project-specific rules that refine the shared workspace conventions.

For substantive work, read `index.md`, `status.md`, and relevant recent
`log.md` entries. Identify the outcome, assess whether the current next action
still advances it, and surface the most important risk or missing decision.
Recommend a specific next step and explain why it matters. Carry authorized
work through verification; preserve evidence and update durable history before
summary state. Keep proposals distinct from agreed decisions.

As the project becomes defined, record or link to its desired outcome,
checkable success criteria, constraints, and important dependencies. Include
time, budget, decision owners, and project-specific approval boundaries when
known. Do not invent these details or require a setup questionnaire before
useful work can begin. Keep this file short and project-specific.
